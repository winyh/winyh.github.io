# A five-minute brief for a personalized song gift

A memorable song gift usually begins with a few precise details. This worksheet helps you prepare those details before you ask a songwriter or use an AI song tool.

**Disclosure:** I am involved with Us in Song, one of the tools linked below. This page is a practical worksheet, not an independent product review.

## The five questions

1. **Who is it for?** Describe the relationship without sharing more personal information than necessary.
2. **What is the occasion?** A birthday, anniversary, wedding or a simple thank-you each suggests a different emotional ending.
3. **Which three details will they recognize?** Try a place, a small shared ritual and a moment you remember differently from anyone else.
4. **What should the chorus say?** Write one sentence that captures the message; make it easy to say out loud.
5. **How should it sound?** Describe tempo, instruments and feeling. For example, “gentle acoustic guitar, relaxed pace, hopeful rather than sentimental.”

## A reusable template

> Write a [style] song for [relationship/occasion]. Include [detail one], [detail two] and [detail three]. The central message is [one sentence]. Start [opening mood], build toward [chorus feeling] and end with [wish or promise]. Avoid personal details that are not needed.

### Example: a friendship birthday

> Write a warm acoustic birthday song for a longtime friend. Mention the rainy bus ride, late cups of tea and the way they make a new city feel familiar. The chorus should say that friendship can make any place feel like home. End with a wish for more small adventures.

### Example: an anniversary

> Write a gentle piano-led song about meeting in a bookshop, walking home in the rain and choosing to show up for each other. Use a conversational tone and end with a hopeful promise. Do not imitate a specific singer.

## Before sending it

- Read the lyrics for invented details, names and pronunciation.
- Listen through the whole audio, including the final seconds.
- Remove private memories or photos the recipient would not want published; ask permission before sharing publicly.
- Use lyrics, images and voices only when you have appropriate rights. AI output is not a guarantee of exclusive rights; check the relevant service terms and applicable law.
- If a tool charges for new generations, check its current quote before revising.
